# Google Stitch UI Design Prompts (5 Core Screens)

Paste these prompts individually into **Google Stitch** (`stitch.google.com`) with **Mobile** selected as the target device.

---

### Design System Prefix (Include at the top of each prompt)
> **Aesthetic:** Modern Editorial Minimalist. Warm alabaster canvas background (`#F7F5F0`), crisp charcoal ink typography (`#18181B`), soft clay card surfaces (`#EFECE6`), subtle 1px hairline borders (`#DED9CE`), and terracotta accent buttons (`#C85A32`). Clean sans-serif UI with editorial serif headers.

---

### Screen 1: Digital Closet (Home)
"Mobile app screen for a personal digital wardrobe named Atelier. Top header with editorial serif title 'My Closet' and a search icon. Below that, a horizontal scrollable row of pill filter chips: 'All (42)', 'Tops', 'Bottoms', 'Outerwear', 'Footwear', 'Accessories' with 'All' active in dark charcoal. Main body is a 2-column grid of rounded cards (`16px` radius) on a soft clay background (`#EFECE6`). Each card displays a centered studio cutout of a clothing item (e.g., vintage denim jacket, white linen shirt, black trousers, leather sneakers) with a subtle drop shadow, brand name in small muted text at bottom-left, and a small pill badge at top-right showing '₹320/wear'. Floating circular '+' action button in terracotta (`#C85A32`) at bottom-right above a 4-tab bottom navigation bar (Closet, Studio, Calendar, Stats)."

### Screen 2: Outfit Shuffle Studio ("Dress Me")
"Mobile outfit creator screen inspired by a digital closet carousel. Top segmented toggle switch between 'Shuffle Mode' (active) and 'Moodboard Canvas'. Main area has three stacked horizontal item carousel rows: Row 1 shows a cropped bomber jacket with left/right peek cards, Row 2 shows pleated wide-leg trousers, Row 3 shows chunky loafers. On the right edge of each row is a circular padlock icon button (Row 2 locked in terracotta, Rows 1 and 3 unlocked). Bottom action bar contains a primary pill button 'Shuffle Outfit' with a dice/sparkles icon, and a secondary outlined button 'Save Look'."

### Screen 3: Freeform Moodboard Canvas
"Mobile interactive outfit collage screen. Top bar with 'Back', outfit title input 'Weekend Coffee Run', and 'Save' button. Center workspace is a large rounded card with a subtle dot-grid pattern on warm alabaster (`#F7F5F0`). Inside the canvas, four clothing cutouts (trench coat, striped knit tee, straight jeans, crossbody bag) are layered aesthetically with subtle selection handles around the trench coat and floating mini controls for 'Bring Forward' and 'Remove'. Bottom horizontal drawer displays small square thumbnails of wardrobe items to tap and drop onto the canvas."

### Screen 4: OOTD Calendar & Trip Packing
"Mobile outfit planner screen. Top segmented control switching between 'OOTD Calendar' (active) and 'Packing Lists'. Below is a clean monthly calendar grid for November with small miniature outfit collage thumbnails inside scheduled date cells. Selected date card at the bottom shows 'Wednesday, Nov 25 — Smart Casual Office' with a preview of the 3 clothing items in that outfit, a green 'Logged as Worn' toggle switch, and a 'Change Outfit' button."

### Screen 5: Wardrobe Analytics & Cost-Per-Wear
"Mobile personal style analytics dashboard. Header 'Closet Insights'. Top 2x2 bento grid of summary cards: 'Total Pieces: 42', 'Closet Value: ₹1,18,500', 'Avg Cost/Wear: ₹410', and 'Utilization: 78%'. Below the bento grid is a section titled 'Best Cost-Per-Wear Gems' featuring a horizontal scroll of item cards with rank badges (#1, #2, #3), item cutouts, 'Worn 28x', and '₹85/wear' highlighted in terracotta. At the bottom is an 'Unworn Alert' card highlighting 5 items untouched in 60+ days."
